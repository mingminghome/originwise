OriginWise Privacy Policy

繁體中文

Last updated: 2026-10-08

OriginWise is a product origin checker that runs in your browser. Your check history and settings stay on your device; checks themselves are processed on our server as described below.

Data stored on your device

  • Settings (language, theme, geo scope, check dimensions)
  • Past check results (text summaries — not full photos)
  • Disclaimer acceptance timestamp

You can delete this data anytime in Settings → Delete local data.

Data sent to our servers

When you run a check, the product name/text and optional compressed photo are sent to our Cloudflare Pages Function, which calls an AI provider (e.g. Google Gemini, OpenAI, xAI, Anthropic) using our server keys. The provider processes the request under its own terms. Requests are rate-limited. We do not create user accounts or keep a server-side history of your checks.

Live web search

When live web search is on, the product name and any barcode (JAN/EAN), including one read from your label photo, are sent to a search service so we can find pages that state where the product is made. The services are Google Search (through Gemini), Brave Search and Firecrawl. They are tried one at a time, Gemini first; the next one is used only if the previous one fails or finds nothing, and a service with no key on this deployment is skipped. A check may therefore reach one or more of them. Our server then fetches a few of the product pages found directly to check the made-in line and barcode (search-results listing pages are never used as evidence). Each search service handles queries under its own privacy policy and may keep its own records of them. Your photo is not sent to search services.

Result cache and server logs

To save quota, the result of a text-only check may be kept in Cloudflare’s edge cache for up to 7 days, stored under a hash of the query, language and settings, and not linked to you or your IP address. Anyone who runs the same query may get that cached result. Checks with a photo are not cached.

Our server logs record a job ID, an IP hash that uses a salt changing daily, and the outcome (result tier or error code). They do not record the product name, the photo or the result text.

Hosting

The app is hosted on Cloudflare Pages. Like most websites, the host may log standard request metadata (for example IP address, URL and user agent) for security and operations. That is separate from your check history, which stays on your device.

Analytics

We may use Google Tag Manager when a container ID is configured for the deployment (build env VITE_GTM_ID). If unset, no GTM script is loaded. Configure GTM/GA in Google’s console for retention and consent as needed.

When GTM is enabled, the app sends screen names (Check, History, Settings, About, How it works) and anonymized actions such as starting or finishing a check, error codes, and support-link clicks. Product names, photos, result text, and error messages are not sent to analytics.

Cookies

The app itself does not require login cookies. Third-party analytics may set cookies according to their policies.

Contact

Open an issue on the project repository for privacy questions.

OriginWise 隱私權政策

English

最後更新：2026-10-08

OriginWise 是在瀏覽器中使用的產品產地查詢工具。查詢紀錄與設定只留在你的裝置；查詢本身會依下文說明，經我們的伺服器處理。

儲存在你裝置上的資料

  • 設定（語言、主題、地區範圍、查詢項目）
  • 過往查詢結果（文字摘要，不含完整照片）
  • 同意免責聲明的時間

你可以隨時在「設定 → 刪除本機資料」中刪除這些資料。

傳送到我們伺服器的資料

進行查詢時，產品名稱／文字及（如有）壓縮後的照片，會傳送到我們的 Cloudflare Pages Function，再以我們的伺服器金鑰呼叫 AI 服務（例如 Google Gemini、OpenAI、xAI、Anthropic）。AI 服務依其自身條款處理請求。請求設有頻率限制。我們不建立使用者帳號，也不在伺服器保存你的查詢紀錄。

即時網路搜尋

開啟即時網路搜尋時，產品名稱及條碼（JAN/EAN，包括從標籤照片讀到的條碼）會傳送給搜尋服務，用來尋找標示產地的網頁。可用的服務包括 Google 搜尋（經 Gemini）、Brave Search 與 Firecrawl：每次只使用一個，Gemini 優先；前一個失敗或找不到結果時才改用下一個，本站未設定金鑰的服務會略過。因此一次查詢可能傳送給其中一個或多個服務。之後，我們的伺服器會直接讀取搜尋找到的少數幾個商品網頁，核對產地標示與條碼（搜尋結果列表頁不會當作依據）。各搜尋服務依其自身隱私權政策處理查詢，並可能自行保留紀錄。你的照片不會傳送給搜尋服務。

結果快取與伺服器日誌

為節省額度，純文字查詢的結果可能存放在 Cloudflare 邊緣快取最多 7 天，以查詢內容、語言與設定的雜湊值作為索引，不會連結到你本人或你的 IP 位址。其他人進行相同查詢時，可能會取得這份快取結果。含照片的查詢不會快取。

伺服器日誌只記錄工作 ID、以每日更換的鹽值雜湊後的 IP，以及查詢結果（分級或錯誤代碼），不會記錄你查詢的產品名稱、照片或結果內容。

網站託管

本 App 託管於 Cloudflare Pages。與大多數網站一樣，託管服務可能為了安全與營運記錄一般請求資料（例如 IP 位址、網址與瀏覽器資訊）。這與你的查詢紀錄無關，查詢紀錄只留在你的裝置。

分析工具

若部署時設定了容器 ID（建置環境變數 VITE_GTM_ID），我們可能使用 Google Tag Manager；未設定時不會載入 GTM。

啟用 GTM 時，App 只會傳送畫面名稱（查詢、紀錄、設定、關於、如何運作），以及開始或完成查詢、錯誤代碼、支援連結點擊等匿名操作。產品名稱、照片、結果內容與錯誤訊息都不會傳送給分析工具。

Cookie

本 App 本身不需要登入 Cookie。第三方分析工具可能依其政策設定 Cookie。

聯絡我們

如有隱私相關問題，請到專案的 GitHub 儲存庫開 Issue。
